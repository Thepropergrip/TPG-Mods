Close DCS. Extract the ZIP and copy the TPG Zolfaghar folder into your OVGME mods folder. Enable it using a configuration rooted at your active Saved Games/DCS folder, then start DCS. Keep Mods and Scripts together; the included visual controller is required. No files are installed into the DCS World folder.
