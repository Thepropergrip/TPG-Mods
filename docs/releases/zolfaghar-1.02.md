TPG Zolfaghar 1.02 · Release Candidate

Twin-missile ballistic launcher for DCS World, with HE and CM versions. Includes inclined launches, animated warhead separation, heat glow, custom infrared materials and a burned destroyed model with shrapnel pitting. The launcher has animated retaining bands, hydraulic rams, stabilizers, suspension, steering and wheels.

Close DCS. Copy the Mods folder into your active Saved Games/DCS/ folder, and copy the contents of the DCS World folder into your DCS World installation folder. Restart DCS. See README.md inside Mods/tech/TPG_Zolfaghar/ for installation details and mission setup, and CREDITS.txt for model attribution.
